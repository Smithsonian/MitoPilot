    This tree shows the NCBI taxonomic lineages of every clade that has a dedicated MitoPilot curation ruleset. Clades shown in blue with a badge are clickable; selecting one displays its curation rules here.

    Use the toggles (▸/▾) to expand and collapse clades, or the filter box to find a taxon. On small screens, open the tree with the ☰ button.
